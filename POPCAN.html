POPCAN

Andrae Hugh Sutherland, known professionally as Popcaan, is a Jamaican dancehall singer, songwriter, and global music ambassador. Rising under Vybz Kartel in the Gaza Music Empire, he broke through with the 2010 hit Clarks. His albums, including Where We Come From, Forever, and Great Is He, blend dancehall with afrobeats, hip-hop, and pop.
